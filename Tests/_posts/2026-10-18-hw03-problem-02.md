### Problem 2. Conditional states and repeated projective measurements (20 points)

#### 문제 공통 조건

**Original**

Use the qutrit projectors from Problem 1. Assume the ideal projective state update

$$
 p_m=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\hat P_m),\qquad
 \hat\rho_m=\frac{\hat P_m\hat\rho\hat P_m}{p_m}
 \quad (p_m>0).
$$

No additional change is made within either measurement subspace. A result of zero probability has no conditional state in that experiment.

**번역**

Problem 1의 큐트릿 사영 연산자 $\hat P_0$, $\hat P_{1,2}$를 사용한다. 위와 같은 이상적인 사영 측정의 상태 갱신 규칙을 가정한다. 각 측정 부분공간 내부에서는 추가적인 상태 변화가 없다. 발생 확률이 $0$인 측정 결과에는 조건부 상태를 정의하지 않는다.

#### 2-1. (4 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

For a pure input $\hat\rho=\lvert \psi\rangle\langle\psi\rvert $, derive the normalized conditional state vector

$$
\lvert \psi_m\rangle=
\frac{\hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle}{\sqrt{\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle}}
\quad (p_m>0).
$$

**번역**

순수 입력 상태 $\hat\rho=\lvert \psi\rangle\langle\psi\rvert $에 대하여 주어진 정규화된 조건부 상태 벡터를 유도하라.

##### [요청 사항]

Born 확률, 사영 후 상태, 정규화 및 $p_m>0$ 조건 유도.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $\langle\psi\rvert \psi\rangle=1$, $\hat P_m^\dagger=\hat P_m=\hat P_m^2$ (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1).

**Step 1. Measurement probability** (Barnett, p. 90, Eq. (4.4))

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_m
&=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\hat P_m)\\
&=\operatorname{Tr}(\lvert \psi\rangle\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m)\\
&=\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2. Unnormalized projection** (Barnett, p. 91, Eqs. (4.7)–(4.8))

$$
\lvert \widetilde\psi_m\rangle=\hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\widetilde\psi_m\rvert \widetilde\psi_m\rangle
&=\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m^\dagger\hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle\\
&=\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle=p_m.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3. Normalize** (Barnett, p. 91, Eq. (4.8))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lvert \psi_m\rangle
&=\frac{\lvert \widetilde\psi_m\rangle}{\sqrt{p_m}}\\
&=\frac{\hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle}{\sqrt{\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle}},
\qquad p_m>0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\langle\psi_m\rvert \psi_m\rangle
=\frac{\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m^2\lvert \psi\rangle}{p_m}=1.
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{\lvert \psi_m\rangle=
\frac{\hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle}{\sqrt{\langle\psi\rvert \hat P_m\lvert \psi\rangle}}
\quad(p_m>0).}
$$

$p_m=0$이면 분모가 $0$이므로 해당 조건부 상태는 정의되지 않는다.

#### 2-2. (12 points)

**Original (공통 지시)**

For each input below, find the probability of each outcome and the normalized conditional state. Use kets for the pure inputs and density operators for the mixed input; give the results in Dirac notation.

**번역 (공통 지시)**

아래 각 입력에 대해 모든 측정 결과의 확률과 정규화된 조건부 상태를 구하라. 순수 입력에는 켓 벡터를, 혼합 입력에는 밀도 연산자를 사용하고, Dirac 표기법으로 답하라.

#### 2-2(a). (2 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

$\lvert \psi\rangle=\lvert 0\rangle$.

**번역**

입력 상태가 $\lvert 0\rangle$이다.

##### [요청 사항]

$p_0$, $p_{1,2}$, 가능한 조건부 상태 및 영확률 결과 처리.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $\hat P_0=\lvert 0\rangle\langle0\rvert $, $\hat P_{1,2}=\lvert 1\rangle\langle1\rvert +\lvert 2\rangle\langle2\rvert $.

**Step 1. Projected vectors** (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1)

$$
\hat P_0\lvert 0\rangle=\lvert 0\rangle,\qquad
\hat P_{1,2}\lvert 0\rangle=0.
$$

**Step 2. Probabilities and states** (Barnett, pp. 90–91, Eqs. (4.4), (4.8))

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_0&=\langle0\rvert \hat P_0\lvert 0\rangle=1,\\
p_{1,2}&=\langle0\rvert \hat P_{1,2}\lvert 0\rangle=0,\\
\lvert \psi_0\rangle&=\lvert 0\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{p_0=1,\quad \lvert \psi_0\rangle=\lvert 0\rangle,\quad p_{1,2}=0.}
$$

결과 $1,2$의 조건부 상태는 정의되지 않는다.

#### 2-2(b). (4 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

$\lvert \psi\rangle=(\lvert 0\rangle+\lvert 1\rangle+\lvert 2\rangle)/\sqrt3$.

**번역**

입력 상태가 세 정규직교 기저 벡터의 동일 진폭 중첩 상태이다.

##### [요청 사항]

두 측정 결과의 확률 및 정규화된 순수 조건부 상태.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $\lvert \psi\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}(\lvert 0\rangle+\lvert 1\rangle+\lvert 2\rangle)$.

**Step 1. Projected vectors** (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0\lvert \psi\rangle&=\frac{\lvert 0\rangle}{\sqrt3},\\
\hat P_{1,2}\lvert \psi\rangle&=\frac{\lvert 1\rangle+\lvert 2\rangle}{\sqrt3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2. Probabilities** (Barnett, p. 90, Eq. (4.4))

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_0&=\frac13,\\
p_{1,2}&=\frac13+\frac13=\frac23,\\
p_0+p_{1,2}&=1.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3. Conditional states** (Barnett, p. 91, Eq. (4.8))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lvert \psi_0\rangle&=\frac{\lvert 0\rangle/\sqrt3}{\sqrt{1/3}}=\lvert 0\rangle,\\
\lvert \psi_{1,2}\rangle
&=\frac{(\lvert 1\rangle+\lvert 2\rangle)/\sqrt3}{\sqrt{2/3}}\\
&=\frac{\lvert 1\rangle+\lvert 2\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
p_0=\frac13,\qquad \lvert \psi_0\rangle=\lvert 0\rangle,\\
p_{1,2}=\frac23,\qquad
\lvert \psi_{1,2}\rangle=\frac{\lvert 1\rangle+\lvert 2\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\end{gathered}}
$$

#### 2-2(c). (6 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

The mixed state

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat\rho
&=\frac14(\lvert 0\rangle+\lvert 1\rangle)(\langle0\rvert +\langle1\rvert )\\
&\quad+\frac14(\lvert 1\rangle-\lvert 2\rangle)(\langle1\rvert -\langle2\rvert ).
\end{aligned}
$$

**번역**

위에 제시된 혼합 상태 $\hat\rho$를 입력으로 사용한다.

##### [요청 사항]

밀도 연산자의 정규화, $p_0$, $p_{1,2}$, $\hat\rho_0$, $\hat\rho_{1,2}$의 Dirac 표기.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $\lvert u\rangle=\lvert 0\rangle+\lvert 1\rangle$, $\lvert v\rangle=\lvert 1\rangle-\lvert 2\rangle$.

$$
\hat\rho=\frac14\lvert u\rangle\langle u\rvert +\frac14\lvert v\rangle\langle v\rvert ,
\qquad\operatorname{Tr}\hat\rho=\frac14(2+2)=1.
$$

**Step 1. Outcome probabilities** (Barnett, p. 90, Eq. (4.4))

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_0&=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\hat P_0)
=\langle0\rvert \hat\rho\lvert 0\rangle=\frac14,\\
p_{1,2}&=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\hat P_{1,2})
=1-p_0=\frac34.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2. Unnormalized conditional operators** (Barnett, p. 91, Eq. (4.7))

$$
\hat P_0\lvert u\rangle=\lvert 0\rangle,\qquad \hat P_0\lvert v\rangle=0.
$$

$$
\hat P_{1,2}\lvert u\rangle=\lvert 1\rangle,\quad
\hat P_{1,2}\lvert v\rangle=\lvert 1\rangle-\lvert 2\rangle.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0\hat\rho\hat P_0
&=\frac14\lvert 0\rangle\langle0\rvert ,\\
\hat P_{1,2}\hat\rho\hat P_{1,2}
&=\frac14\lvert 1\rangle\langle1\rvert \\
&\quad+\frac14(\lvert 1\rangle-\lvert 2\rangle)(\langle1\rvert -\langle2\rvert ).
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3. Normalize** (Barnett, p. 91, Eq. (4.8))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat\rho_0
&=\frac{\hat P_0\hat\rho\hat P_0}{p_0}=\lvert 0\rangle\langle0\rvert ,\\
\hat\rho_{1,2}
&=\frac{\hat P_{1,2}\hat\rho\hat P_{1,2}}{p_{1,2}}\\
&=\frac13\lvert 1\rangle\langle1\rvert \\
&\quad+\frac13(\lvert 1\rangle-\lvert 2\rangle)(\langle1\rvert -\langle2\rvert )\\
&=\frac23\lvert 1\rangle\langle1\rvert +\frac13\lvert 2\rangle\langle2\rvert \\
&\quad-\frac13(\lvert 1\rangle\langle2\rvert +\lvert 2\rangle\langle1\rvert ).
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho_0)=1,
\qquad \operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho_{1,2})=1.
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
p_0=\frac14,\quad \hat\rho_0=\lvert 0\rangle\langle0\rvert ,\\
p_{1,2}=\frac34,\\
\hat\rho_{1,2}=\frac13\lvert 1\rangle\langle1\rvert 
+\frac13(\lvert 1\rangle-\lvert 2\rangle)(\langle1\rvert -\langle2\rvert ).
\end{gathered}}
$$

#### 2-3. (4 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

Immediately repeat the same ideal measurement on the same qutrit, with no change in its state between the two measurements. If the first result was $m$ with $p_m>0$, use density operators and projectors to show that the second result is also $m$ with certainty and that the conditional state is unchanged.

**번역**

동일한 큐트릿에 같은 이상적 측정을 상태 변화 없이 즉시 반복한다. 첫 결과가 $m$이며 $p_m>0$일 때, 두 번째 결과 역시 반드시 $m$이고 조건부 상태가 변하지 않음을 밀도 연산자와 사영 연산자를 사용해 증명하라.

##### [요청 사항]

반복 측정 확률 $P(k\mid m)$, 확률 $1$의 재현성, 반복 후 상태 불변성.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $\hat\rho_m=\hat P_m\hat\rho\hat P_m/p_m$, $\hat P_k\hat P_m=\delta_{km}\hat P_m$ (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1; p. 91, Eq. (4.8)).

**Step 1. First post-measurement state** (Barnett, p. 91, Eq. (4.8))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_m\hat\rho_m\hat P_m
&=\frac{\hat P_m^2\hat\rho\hat P_m^2}{p_m}\\
&=\frac{\hat P_m\hat\rho\hat P_m}{p_m}=\hat\rho_m.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2. Repeated outcome** (Barnett, p. 90, Eq. (4.4))

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(k\mid m)
&=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho_m\hat P_k)\\
&=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(\hat P_m\hat\rho\hat P_m\hat P_k)}{p_m}\\
&=\delta_{km}\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(\hat P_m\hat\rho\hat P_m)}{p_m}\\
&=\delta_{km}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\implies P(m\mid m)=1,\qquad P(k\ne m\mid m)=0.
$$

**Step 3. Second conditional state** (Barnett, p. 91, Eq. (4.8))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat\rho_m^{(2)}
&=\frac{\hat P_m\hat\rho_m\hat P_m}{P(m\mid m)}\\
&=\frac{\hat\rho_m}{1}=\hat\rho_m.
\end{aligned}
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{P(k\mid m)=\delta_{km},\qquad \hat\rho_m^{(2)}=\hat\rho_m.}
$$
