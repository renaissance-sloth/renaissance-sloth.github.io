### Problem 5. Minimum-error discrimination of two pure states (10 points)

#### 문제 공통 조건

**Original**

Alice prepares either of two known states with equal prior probabilities $p_1=p_2=1/2$:

$$
\lvert \psi_1\rangle=\cos\theta\lvert 0\rangle+\sin\theta\lvert 1\rangle,\qquad
\lvert \psi_2\rangle=\cos\theta\lvert 0\rangle-\sin\theta\lvert 1\rangle,
$$

where $0<\theta<\pi/4$. Define $\hat\rho_j=\lvert \psi_j\rangle\langle\psi_j\rvert $. Every measurement outcome must be assigned to one of the two input states; inconclusive results are not allowed. You may use the lecture result: an optimal measurement can be chosen as projectors onto the positive- and negative-eigenvalue subspaces of

$$
\hat\Delta=p_1\hat\rho_1-p_2\hat\rho_2.
$$

The positive outcome is assigned to state 1 and the negative outcome to state 2. No proof of the general optimality theorem is required.

**번역**

Alice는 두 상태 $\lvert \psi_1\rangle$, $\lvert \psi_2\rangle$ 중 하나를 각각 $1/2$의 사전확률로 준비한다. $0<\theta<\pi/4$이고 $\hat\rho_j=\lvert \psi_j\rangle\langle\psi_j\rvert $이다. 모든 측정 결과는 두 입력 상태 중 하나에 할당해야 하며, 판별 불가 결과는 허용되지 않는다. $\hat\Delta=p_1\hat\rho_1-p_2\hat\rho_2$의 양·음 고유값 부분공간에 사영하는 측정이 최적이라는 강의 결과를 사용해도 된다. 일반적인 최적성 정리의 증명은 필요 없다.

#### 5-1. (5 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

Find $\hat\Delta$, its eigenvalues and normalized eigenvectors, and the optimal projectors $\hat\pi_1$, $\hat\pi_2$. Use the ordered basis $(\lvert 0\rangle,\lvert 1\rangle)$.

**번역**

순서 있는 기저 $(\lvert 0\rangle,\lvert 1\rangle)$에서 $\hat\Delta$와 그 고유값 및 정규화된 고유벡터, 최적 측정의 사영 연산자 $\hat\pi_1$, $\hat\pi_2$를 구하라.

##### [요청 사항]

Helstrom 차이 연산자, 고유값·고유벡터, 최적 사영 측정.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $c=\cos\theta$, $s=\sin\theta$, $0<s<c$, $p_1=p_2=1/2$.

**Step 1. Density matrices** (Barnett, p. 101, Eq. (4.56))

$$
\hat\rho_1=
\begin{pmatrix}c^2&cs\\cs&s^2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\hat\rho_2=
\begin{pmatrix}c^2&-cs\\-cs&s^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Step 2. Difference operator** (Barnett, p. 101, text preceding Eq. (4.57))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat\Delta
&=\frac12(\hat\rho_1-\hat\rho_2)\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}0&cs\\cs&0\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\frac12\sin(2\theta)\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3. Eigenvalues and normalized eigenvectors** (Barnett, §4.4, p. 101)

$$
\det(\hat\Delta-\lambda\hat I)=\lambda^2-c^2s^2=0.
$$

$$
\lambda_+=cs=\frac12\sin2\theta>0,\qquad
\lambda_-=-cs=-\frac12\sin2\theta<0.
$$

$$
\lvert +\rangle=\frac{\lvert 0\rangle+\lvert 1\rangle}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\lvert -\rangle=\frac{\lvert 0\rangle-\lvert 1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

$$
\hat\Delta\lvert +\rangle=\lambda_+\lvert +\rangle,\qquad
\hat\Delta\lvert -\rangle=\lambda_-\lvert -\rangle.
$$

**Step 4. Optimal projectors** (Barnett, p. 101, Eq. (4.57))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat\pi_1&=\lvert +\rangle\langle+\rvert 
=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\\
\hat\pi_2&=\lvert -\rangle\langle-\rvert 
=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hat\pi_1+\hat\pi_2=\hat I,\qquad
\hat\pi_i\hat\pi_j=\delta_{ij}\hat\pi_i.
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\hat\Delta=\frac12\sin2\theta\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\\
\lambda_\pm=\pm\frac12\sin2\theta,\qquad
\lvert \pm\rangle=\frac{\lvert 0\rangle\pm\lvert 1\rangle}{\sqrt2},\\
\hat\pi_1=\lvert +\rangle\langle+\rvert ,\qquad
\hat\pi_2=\lvert -\rangle\langle-\rvert .
\end{gathered}}
$$

#### 5-2. (5 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

Calculate the average error probability from the two possible wrong assignments. Express the result as a function of $\theta$, and explain its limits as $\theta\to0$ and $\theta\to\pi/4$.

**번역**

두 가지 오판별 가능성으로부터 평균 오류 확률을 계산하고, 이를 $\theta$의 함수로 나타내라. $\theta\to0$ 및 $\theta\to\pi/4$에서의 극한을 설명하라.

##### [요청 사항]

두 오판별 확률, 평균 최소 오류 확률, 두 물리적 극한.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.** $P_{2\mid 1}=\langle\psi_1\rvert \hat\pi_2\lvert \psi_1\rangle$, $P_{1\mid 2}=\langle\psi_2\rvert \hat\pi_1\lvert \psi_2\rangle$.

**Step 1. Wrong assignment for input 1** (Barnett, p. 100, Eq. (4.46); p. 101, Eq. (4.57))

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{2\mid 1}
&=\lvert \langle-\rvert \psi_1\rangle\rvert ^2\\
&=\left\lvert\frac{\cos\theta-\sin\theta}{\sqrt2}\right\rvert^2\\
&=\frac12(1-\sin2\theta).
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2. Wrong assignment for input 2** (Barnett, p. 100, Eq. (4.46))

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{1\mid 2}
&=\lvert \langle+\rvert \psi_2\rangle\rvert ^2\\
&=\left\lvert\frac{\cos\theta-\sin\theta}{\sqrt2}\right\rvert^2\\
&=\frac12(1-\sin2\theta).
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3. Average error** (Barnett, p. 102, Eq. (4.58))

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{err}}
&=\frac12P_{2\mid 1}+\frac12P_{1\mid 2}\\
&=\frac12\left(\cos\theta-\sin\theta\right)^2\\
&=\frac12\left[1-\sin(2\theta)\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 4. Limiting cases** (Barnett, p. 102, Eq. (4.58))

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta\to0&:\quad \lvert \psi_1\rangle\to\lvert 0\rangle,
\ \lvert \psi_2\rangle\to\lvert 0\rangle,\quad P_{\mathrm{err}}\to\frac12,\\
\theta\to\frac\pi4&:\quad\langle\psi_1\rvert \psi_2\rangle\to0,
\quad P_{\mathrm{err}}\to0.
\end{aligned}
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{P_{\mathrm{err}}^{\min}(\theta)=\frac12[1-\sin(2\theta)].}
$$

$\theta\to0$이면 두 상태가 같아져 무작위 추측의 오류율 $1/2$에 접근한다. $\theta\to\pi/4$이면 두 상태가 서로 직교하여 오류 없이 판별할 수 있다.
