### Problem 1. Projectors and nonunitary projection (10 points)

#### 문제 공통 조건

**Original**

A qutrit has the orthonormal basis $\lbrace \lvert 0\rangle, \lvert 1\rangle, \lvert 2\rangle \rbrace$. Consider the two measurement projectors

$$
\hat P_0 = \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert , \qquad
\hat P_{1,2} = \lvert 1\rangle\langle 1\rvert  + \lvert 2\rangle\langle 2\rvert .
$$

The outcome labeled $1,2$ selects the subspace spanned by $\lvert 1\rangle$ and $\lvert 2\rangle$, without distinguishing these states.

**번역**

큐트릿은 정규직교 기저 $\lbrace \lvert 0\rangle, \lvert 1\rangle, \lvert 2\rangle \rbrace$를 가진다. 위의 두 측정 사영 연산자를 고려하라. 측정 결과 $1,2$는 두 상태를 구별하지 않고 $\lvert 1\rangle$과 $\lvert 2\rangle$가 생성하는 부분공간을 선택한다.

#### 1-1. (5 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

Verify that the projectors are Hermitian and positive, sum to $\hat I$, and satisfy $\hat P_j\hat P_k = \delta_{jk}\hat P_j$, where $j$ and $k$ label the two outcomes. Use their action on an arbitrary qutrit vector to show positivity.

**번역**

두 사영 연산자가 에르미트이고 양의 준정부호이며, 합이 항등 연산자 $\hat I$이고, $\hat P_j\hat P_k = \delta_{jk}\hat P_j$를 만족함을 검증하라. 임의의 큐트릿 벡터에 대한 작용을 이용하여 양의 준정부호임을 보여라.

##### [요청 사항]

Hermiticity, positivity, completeness, orthogonality 및 idempotence 검증.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.**

$$
\lvert \phi\rangle = a_0\lvert 0\rangle + a_1\lvert 1\rangle + a_2\lvert 2\rangle,
\qquad a_j \in \mathbb C.
$$

$$
\langle j\rvert k\rangle=\delta_{jk},
\qquad\sum_{j=0}^2 \lvert j\rangle\langle j\rvert =\hat I.
$$

**Step 1. Hermiticity** (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1, I)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0^\dagger
&= (\lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert )^\dagger \\
&= \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert  = \hat P_0, \\
\hat P_{1,2}^\dagger
&= (\lvert 1\rangle\langle 1\rvert  + \lvert 2\rangle\langle 2\rvert )^\dagger \\
&= \lvert 1\rangle\langle 1\rvert  + \lvert 2\rangle\langle 2\rvert  \\
&= \hat P_{1,2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore\quad \hat P_j^\dagger = \hat P_j.
$$

**Step 2. Positivity** (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1, II)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0\lvert \phi\rangle
&= a_0\lvert 0\rangle, \\
\hat P_{1,2}\lvert \phi\rangle
&= a_1\lvert 1\rangle + a_2\lvert 2\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\phi\rvert \hat P_0\lvert \phi\rangle
&= \lvert a_0\rvert ^2 \geq 0, \\
\langle\phi\rvert \hat P_{1,2}\lvert \phi\rangle
&= \lvert a_1\rvert ^2 + \lvert a_2\rvert ^2 \geq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore\quad \hat P_0 \geq 0,
\qquad \hat P_{1,2} \geq 0.
$$

**Step 3. Completeness** (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1, III)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0 + \hat P_{1,2}
&= \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert  + \lvert 1\rangle\langle 1\rvert  \\
&\quad + \lvert 2\rangle\langle 2\rvert  \\
&= \hat I.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 4. Orthogonality and idempotence** (Barnett, p. 90, Table 4.1, IV)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0^2
&= \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert  \\
&= \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert  = \hat P_0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_{1,2}^2
&= \sum_{j,k=1}^{2}\lvert j\rangle\langle j\rvert k\rangle\langle k\rvert  \\
&= \sum_{j,k=1}^{2}\delta_{jk}\lvert j\rangle\langle k\rvert  \\
&= \hat P_{1,2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0\hat P_{1,2}
&= \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert 1\rangle\langle 1\rvert  \\
&\quad + \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert 2\rangle\langle 2\rvert  \\
&= 0, \\
\hat P_{1,2}\hat P_0 &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore\quad \hat P_j\hat P_k = \delta_{jk}\hat P_j.
$$

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\hat P_j^\dagger = \hat P_j, \qquad \hat P_j \geq 0, \\
\hat P_0 + \hat P_{1,2} = \hat I, \\
\hat P_j\hat P_k = \delta_{jk}\hat P_j.
\end{gathered}
}
$$

#### 1-2. (5 points)

##### [문제 원문 및 번역]

**Original**

For a normalized input $\lvert \chi\rangle = c_0\lvert 0\rangle + c_1\lvert 1\rangle + c_2\lvert 2\rangle$, find the squared norm after applying each projector. Compare $\hat P_j^\dagger\hat P_j$ with the unitary condition. Does normalizing the projected vector make the resulting transformation unitary on the full qutrit state space? Explain briefly.

**번역**

정규화된 입력 상태 $\lvert \chi\rangle$에 각 사영 연산자를 적용한 뒤의 노름 제곱을 구하라. $\hat P_j^\dagger\hat P_j$를 유니터리 조건과 비교하라. 사영된 벡터를 정규화하면 전체 큐트릿 상태공간에서 유니터리 변환이 되는가? 간단히 설명하라.

##### [요청 사항]

Projection norm, unitary condition, normalization 후 unitary 여부.

##### [풀이 과정 (Symbolic & Referenced)]

**Def.**

$$
\begin{gathered}
\lvert \chi\rangle = c_0\lvert 0\rangle + c_1\lvert 1\rangle + c_2\lvert 2\rangle, \\
\lvert c_0\rvert ^2 + \lvert c_1\rvert ^2 + \lvert c_2\rvert ^2 = 1.
\end{gathered}
$$

**Step 1. Squared norms** (Barnett, p. 90, Eq. (4.4) 및 Table 4.1)

$$
\hat P_0\lvert \chi\rangle = c_0\lvert 0\rangle.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigl\lVert \hat P_0\lvert \chi\rangle \bigr\rVert^2
&= \langle\chi\rvert \hat P_0^\dagger\hat P_0\lvert \chi\rangle \\
&= \lvert c_0\rvert ^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hat P_{1,2}\lvert \chi\rangle = c_1\lvert 1\rangle + c_2\lvert 2\rangle.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigl\lVert \hat P_{1,2}\lvert \chi\rangle \bigr\rVert^2
&= \langle\chi\rvert \hat P_{1,2}^\dagger\hat P_{1,2}\lvert \chi\rangle \\
&= \lvert c_1\rvert ^2 + \lvert c_2\rvert ^2 \\
&= 1 - \lvert c_0\rvert ^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Born rule: $P(\lambda_n) = \operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\hat P_n)$ (Barnett, p. 90, Eq. (4.4)).

**Step 2. Unitary condition** (Barnett, p. 36, Eq. (2.37))

유니터리 연산자 $\hat U$는 $\hat U^\dagger\hat U = \hat I$를 만족해야 한다. 그러나

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P_0^\dagger\hat P_0
&= \hat P_0^2 = \hat P_0 \neq \hat I, \\
\hat P_{1,2}^\dagger\hat P_{1,2}
&= \hat P_{1,2}^2 = \hat P_{1,2} \neq \hat I.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore\quad
\hat P_0,\ \hat P_{1,2}\ \text{are nonunitary.}
$$

**Step 3. Normalized projected states** (Barnett, p. 91, Eqs. (4.7)–(4.8))

$\lvert c_0\rvert ^2 > 0$일 때,

$$
\lvert \chi_0\rangle
= \frac{\hat P_0\lvert \chi\rangle}{\sqrt{\lvert c_0\rvert ^2}}
= \frac{c_0}{\lvert c_0\rvert }\lvert 0\rangle.
$$

$\lvert c_1\rvert ^2 + \lvert c_2\rvert ^2 > 0$일 때,

$$
\lvert \chi_{1,2}\rangle
= \frac{c_1\lvert 1\rangle+c_2\lvert 2\rangle}{\sqrt{\lvert c_1\rvert ^2+\lvert c_2\rvert ^2}}.
$$

**Step 4. Inner-product preservation** (Barnett, p. 44, Eq. (2.79))

다음 두 직교 상태를 고려하자.

$$
\lvert \chi_\pm\rangle
= \frac{\lvert 0\rangle\pm\lvert 1\rangle}{\sqrt 2},
\qquad \langle\chi_+\rvert \chi_-\rangle=0.
$$

하지만 $\hat P_0$로 사영한 후 각각 정규화하면

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\hat P_0\lvert \chi_+\rangle}{\bigl\lVert\hat P_0\lvert \chi_+\rangle\bigr\rVert}
&= \lvert 0\rangle, \\
\frac{\hat P_0\lvert \chi_-\rangle}{\bigl\lVert\hat P_0\lvert \chi_-\rangle\bigr\rVert}
&= \lvert 0\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

따라서

$$
\langle\chi_{+,0}\rvert \chi_{-,0}\rangle = 1 \neq 0.
$$

정규화 후에도 내적이 보존되지 않으므로 전체 큐트릿 공간의 유니터리 변환일 수 없다. 또한 해당 사영 결과의 확률이 $0$이면 정규화 자체가 정의되지 않는다.

##### [최종 답안]

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\bigl\lVert \hat P_0\lvert \chi\rangle \bigr\rVert^2 &= \lvert c_0\rvert ^2, \\
\bigl\lVert \hat P_{1,2}\lvert \chi\rangle \bigr\rVert^2 &= \lvert c_1\rvert ^2+\lvert c_2\rvert ^2, \\
\hat P_j^\dagger\hat P_j &= \hat P_j \neq \hat I.
\end{aligned}
}
$$

**결론:** 정규화는 사영을 유니터리 변환으로 만들지 않는다. 사영은 입력 상태의 일부 성분을 소거하므로, 정규화하더라도 전체 공간에서 가역성과 내적 보존 조건을 만족하지 않는다.
